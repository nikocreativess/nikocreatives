Clipcaster

A desktop app for Windows that adds captions to your gameplay clips and posts them to TikTok and YouTube Shorts.

How it works

  1. Pick a gameplay clip on your computer.
  2. Clipcaster generates captions and lets you edit them.
  3. Connect your TikTok or YouTube account and post the clip directly, or send it to your TikTok drafts to finish in the app.

Your accounts, your control

Clipcaster runs on your own computer. It only posts when you tell it to, and you can disconnect an account at any time from the app or from your TikTok and Google account settings.
